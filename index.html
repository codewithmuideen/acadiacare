Who We Are

Real people. Real care. Real difference.

Acadia Support Services Limited is based at Whittle Rise on Staffordshire Technology Park in Stafford. We have been delivering high quality domiciliary care since 2013 and we have grown steadily because our service genuinely works.

We are a CQC registered provider. That means every aspect of our care meets the standards set by the national regulator. Our team supports adults of all ages living with a wide range of conditions including dementia, Parkinson's, stroke, cancer, learning disabilities, mental health needs and physical disabilities.

We also recognise that care is deeply personal. So we accommodate dietary requirements, cultural and religious needs, and we offer both male and female carers. Our staff are pet friendly too because we know how much an animal companion can mean to someone.

Family Share

“I’m fine, darling.”

You can’t always be there. But you can always stay connected.

Three words every son and daughter wants to believe. But when Mum or Dad is living with dementia, or simply prefers not to worry you, how do you really know they have eaten, taken their medication and are comfortable at home?

We believe families deserve more than reassurance. They deserve transparency. Alongside compassionate care, our digital care management system lets authorised family members see care notes and visit updates, whether they are at work, travelling or living miles away.

  • TrustVetted, DBS checked carers matched to your loved one, with the continuity that builds real relationships.
  • TransparencySee what happened at each visit: meals, medication support, mood and wellbeing, recorded by the carer.
  • Peace of mindKeep working, travelling and living your life without constantly wondering how Mum is doing.

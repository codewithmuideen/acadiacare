Where We Started

A small team with a big purpose

Acadia Support Services Limited was established in 2013 with one clear aim: to provide the kind of care we would want for the people we love. Not box ticking. Not rushed visits. Real, thoughtful, person centred care.

We are based at Whittle Rise on Staffordshire Technology Park in Stafford and we have grown steadily because families and organisations in our area trust us to deliver. That trust has been earned through consistency, honesty and a team that genuinely cares about what they do.

Today we support adults of all ages living with a wide range of conditions across Staffordshire. Our services reach from an hourly home visit right through to full time live in nursing care. Whatever someone needs, we find a way to make it work.

We are also proud of how we run our team. We pay our staff competitively, offer real flexibility and support their development. When carers feel valued, they do better work and the people they support feel that.

Our Mission and Values

Everything we do comes back to these three things. If we are not living up to them, we want to know.

Compassion

Every single visit, phone call and interaction is guided by genuine warmth. We care about the people we work with, not just the task in front of us.

Quality

CQC registered and internally quality assured. We hold ourselves to a high standard and we do not cut corners, ever.

Partnership

We work closely with service users, families, GPs and commissioners. Good care is never done in isolation. It takes everyone working together.

Our Promises

What you can always count on

Whether you are a service user, a family member or an organisation, here is what Acadia Care commits to every time.

CQC Registered

We are regulated by the Care Quality Commission and we meet the national standards expected of high quality care providers.

Thoroughly Vetted Staff

Every single member of our team is enhanced DBS checked, fully referenced and trained before they ever visit a service user.

Rapid Response

Care emergencies cannot wait. We respond quickly and can often place a qualified carer within hours of your call.

Culturally Inclusive

Halal, Kosher, vegan and faith sensitive care. Male or female carers. LGBTQ+ welcoming. Pet friendly. We celebrate what makes each person unique.

Flexible Funding

Self funding, Local Authority and NHS Continuing Healthcare all accepted. We will help you find the right funding route for your situation.

Always Person Centred

We take the time to understand the whole person before any care begins. Their needs, preferences, routines, culture and what actually matters to them.

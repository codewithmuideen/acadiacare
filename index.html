Who We Are

Real people. Real care. Real difference.

Acadia Support Services Limited is based at Whittle Rise on Staffordshire Technology Park in Stafford. We have been delivering high quality domiciliary care since 2013 and we have grown steadily because our service genuinely works.

We are a CQC registered provider. That means every aspect of our care meets the standards set by the national regulator. Our team supports adults of all ages living with a wide range of conditions including dementia, Parkinson's, stroke, cancer, learning disabilities, mental health needs and physical disabilities.

We also recognise that care is deeply personal. So we accommodate dietary requirements, cultural and religious needs, and we offer both male and female carers. Our staff are pet friendly too because we know how much an animal companion can mean to someone.

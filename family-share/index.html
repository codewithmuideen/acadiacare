Finding trustworthy dementia care or home care in Staffordshire can be one of the most emotional decisions a family ever makes.

“I’m fine, darling.”

When an elderly parent says those words, we want to believe them. But for families supporting someone living with dementia, they may not always tell the full story.

Has Mum eaten today? Has she taken her medication? Is she comfortable, happy and getting the support she needs? When you are at work, travelling or living miles away, these questions can follow you around all day.

Why “I’m fine” is not always the whole picture

Many older people play down their needs. Some don’t want to worry their children. Some are proud and fiercely independent. For someone living with dementia, it may simply be hard to remember whether lunch was eaten or tablets were taken. None of this is anyone’s fault, but it can leave families guessing.

What good home care should give you

  • Trust: knowing exactly who is coming into your parent’s home, that they are vetted and DBS checked, and that they will turn up.
  • Transparency: being able to see that the care you have arranged is actually happening, visit by visit.
  • Peace of mind: being able to carry on working, travelling and living your own life without constant worry.

How Acadia Family Share keeps you informed

At Acadia Support Services we combine compassionate, personalised home care with digital care management technology. After each visit, your loved one’s carer records what happened. Through our secure Family Share feature, authorised relatives can read those care notes and updates from their phone, wherever they are.

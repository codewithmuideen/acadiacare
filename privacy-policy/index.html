Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

Who We Are

Acadia Support Services Ltd ("Acadia Care", "we") is a CQC registered healthcare and social care provider based at Whittle Rise, Staffordshire Technology Park, Stafford, ST18 0ES. We are the data controller for the personal information described here. To contact us about data protection, email care@acadiacare.co.uk or call 07949 720 932.

What We Collect

We collect personal information when you contact us, apply for work or enquire about our services. This can include your name, address, email, phone number, employment history, qualifications, right to work status, DBS information, your CV and supporting documents, health information relevant to care delivery and financial information where relevant.

How We Use It

We use your information to respond to enquiries, deliver care services, assess job applications, comply with CQC and other legal obligations and send you communications you would expect, such as a confirmation that we have received your message or application.

Legal Basis

We process personal data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 on the basis of taking steps at your request before entering a contract, performance of a contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests and, where required, your explicit consent.

How Our Website Handles Your Data

When you submit a form on this website, your details are sent securely (encrypted in transit) to our systems. Enquiries and applications are stored in a secure database and documents you upload are kept in private storage that only authorised Acadia Care staff can access. We use trusted processors to run this: Supabase (database and file storage), Resend (sending emails) and our website host. Each is bound by a data processing agreement.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share it with care commissioners, local authorities, NHS bodies, the DBS service and insurers where necessary. All third parties are bound by appropriate data protection agreements.

Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary. Unsuccessful job applications, including CVs and documents, are normally deleted after 6 months. Service user records follow CQC and NHS records management guidance.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your data, and the right to data portability. Contact care@acadiacare.co.uk to exercise them. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

WhatsApp

When you contact us via WhatsApp we may store messages to allow us to respond. We do not use WhatsApp for unsolicited marketing.

Cookies

This website uses only essential browser storage needed for it to work (for example, remembering page content so it loads faster, and keeping authorised staff signed in). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be on this page with the date shown above.

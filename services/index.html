In Your Own Home

Domiciliary and Home Care

Our domiciliary care service allows people to stay in the home they know and love, on their own terms. Whether someone needs a carer to visit each morning or someone to move in and be there around the clock, we build a plan that fits around their life, not the other way around.

We carefully match each person with a carer who suits their personality, needs and preferences. That continuity matters enormously and we work hard to maintain it.

  • Personal care, washing, dressing and grooming
  • Oral and intravenous medication support
  • Meal preparation and nutrition
  • Companionship and social engagement
  • Visits from as little as 30 minutes
